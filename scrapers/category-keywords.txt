STANDARD CATEGORY KEYWORD MAPPING
==================================
Used across all scrapers to infer categories from product names.
Defined in scrapers/categories.ts (12 standard categories).
First keyword match wins — order matters.

brace, subframe, chassis, control arm, camber, trailing arm, monoball,
